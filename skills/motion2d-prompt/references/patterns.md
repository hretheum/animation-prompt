# Optional modules

> Phrases quoted in this file are illustrations. Write every prompt sentence in your own words.

Scope bans · 11. Tone calibration · 12. Poster frame · 13. Accumulation looks · 14. Implementation hygiene · 15. Closing formulas

1. **Readable beats**: break any big moment into 3–5 named beats readable at full speed ("GATHER / HOLD / BURST / SETTLE").
2. **Signature move**: one motion that defines the piece (a line that never lifts; letters that fall up; shapes that fold like paper), described in frames and curves, reused in every section.
3. **Object constancy**: every element keeps its identity across states; nothing disappears and reappears when it could travel.
4. **Match-cut chain**: every scene hands over to the next through a shared shape; the whole film is one continuous transformation.
5. **Layered parallax**: 3–6 flat layers moving at different rates for depth without 3D; atmospheric colour shift per layer.
6. **Cursor as actor**: the pointer is a character in the piece (shapes notice it, flee, follow, orbit); define its influence radius and falloff.
7. **Diegetic state**: show progress or state through the artwork (a loader that is a tide filling; a scroll position that is a sunrise), never a bar unless the piece is a bar.
8. **Sound sync**: procedural Web Audio or analysis of a provided track; beats drive accents; the "mute test" passes; audio unlocks on first gesture.
9. **Contrast amplifier**: the emotional contrast that powers the payoff (stillness → explosion; order → chaos → new order).
10. **Scope bans**: forbid additions that dilute the idea: "no confetti, no particles for decoration, no gradient blobs, no extra logos".
11. **Tone calibration**: for serious subjects, restraint over spectacle; say where (if anywhere) a shock is allowed.
12. **Poster frame**: designate the exact frame (or seed) used as thumbnail/OG image and make it exportable as PNG/SVG.
13. **Accumulation looks**: paint-like build-up, trails and feedback; define fade rate, reset rules and how export reproduces them deterministically.
14. **Implementation hygiene**: time as pure function, seeded randomness, offscreen pause, dispose on resize, DPR clamp, no layout thrash, adaptive render scale.
15. **Closing formulas**: "the most [X] [Y], finally [impossible verb]"; "everyone has [seen X]; this is the first [Y] that [Z]"; the compulsion line ("…and you will watch it loop until you forget why you opened the tab").
